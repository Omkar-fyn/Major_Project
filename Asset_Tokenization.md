# Problem Statements

### Asset-Tokenization : Block-Chain based token to simplfy Investing and access For Capital

1. What is the problem?

    Many people want to invest in stable, income-generating assets like real estate, warehouses, or manufacturing units, but the entry barrier is very high. These assets usually require large capital, legal paperwork, and long-term commitments.

    On the other side, small and medium business owners often struggle to raise capital. Their only options are bank loans, high-interest NBFC loans, or giving away equity to investors. This either increases their financial pressure or reduces their ownership before the business even stabilizes.

    So there is a gap:
        Investors want safe, real-world assets.
        Businesses need capital.
        But both sides cannot easily connect in a transparent and efficient way.

2. Who faces this problem?

    Middle-class and upper-middle-class individuals who want to invest in real assets but cannot afford full ownership.

    Young professionals who want diversified investments beyond stocks and mutual funds.

    Small and medium business owners who need capital but don’t want heavy debt or major equity dilution.

    Real estate or infrastructure developers who need funding for specific projects.

3. How serious is it? 

    This is a large-scale economic issue.
    Real estate and industrial assets require very high upfront capital.
    Most individuals can only invest in financial instruments like stocks or mutual funds, not physical assets.
    Small businesses often shut down because of high loan interest and cash-flow pressure.
    A large portion of personal income goes toward rent, EMIs, and basic expenses, leaving little room for asset ownership.

    If capital access and asset ownership become easier, it can:
        Increase small business growth.
        Improve financial inclusion.
        Strengthen the overall economy.

4. What solutions already exist?

    There are some existing solutions:

    1. Bank loans and NBFC financing
    Businesses borrow money and repay with interest.

    2. Equity funding from investors or VCs
    Founders give away a percentage of ownership.

    3. REITs (Real Estate Investment Trusts)
    People can invest in real estate through the stock market.

    4. Crowdfunding platforms
    Some platforms allow pooled investments into projects.

    5. Global asset-tokenization platforms
    A few startups tokenize real estate or other assets using blockchain.

5. What are the limitations of existing solutions?

    Loans create financial pressure due to interest and fixed repayment schedules.
    Equity funding leads to ownership dilution and loss of control.
    REITs are limited to large, regulated assets and not accessible for smaller projects.
    Crowdfunding often lacks liquidity and transparency.
    Existing tokenization platforms are mostly outside India or focus on large institutional investors.

    In short:
    Entry barriers are still high.
    Liquidity is limited.
    Access for retail investors is restricted.
    Regulatory clarity is still evolving in many regions.

6. What is your proposed approach?

    I want to build a platform that tokenizes real-world assets and allows people to invest in fractional ownership.

    The idea is:
    A business or asset owner lists a real asset on the platform.
    The asset is legally structured and represented as digital tokens.
    Investors can buy small portions of that asset instead of the whole thing.
    Investors earn returns based on the asset’s performance, such as rent or profits.
    Tokens can be traded on a secondary marketplace for liquidity.
    
    This creates a win-win system:
    Businesses get capital without heavy loans.
    Investors get access to real assets with smaller investment amounts.

7. What technology will you use?

    Core technology stack:

    Backend:
    Node.js
    MongoDB

    Frontend:
    Next.js

    Blockchain layer:
    Ethereum for smart contracts
    ERC-20 or ERC-1400 style token standards for asset tokens

    Smart contracts:
    Solidity for token issuance and transfers
    Contracts for dividend or rent distribution

    Infrastructure:
    Cloud hosting (AWS, GCP, or similar)
    IPFS or secure storage for asset documents

8. Is the solution feasible in 4–6 months?

    User registration and KYC simulation
    Asset listing interface
    Token issuance via smart contracts
    Investor dashboard
    Basic secondary trading simulation

9. What is the expected impact or outcome?

    1. For investors:
    Access to real-world assets with small capital.
    Diversified investment options.
    Potential passive income streams.

    2. For businesses:
    Easier access to capital.
    Less dependency on high-interest loans.
    More flexible funding structures.

    3. For the economy:
    Better capital flow into small and mid-scale assets.
    Increased participation in asset ownership.
    More transparent investment ecosystems.
