IEEE RESEARCH PAPER HANDBOOK

Asset Tokenization Platform Using Ethereum Blockchain

Version 1.0

================================================================== PART
1 – RESEARCH MINDSET
==================================================================

A research paper is not a project report.

A project report explains what you built. A research paper explains: -
Why the problem matters. - What previous researchers have done. - What
limitations exist. - What you propose. - Why your approach is better or
different. - Evidence supporting your claims.

Every paragraph should answer one of these questions: 1. What is the
problem? 2. Why does it exist? 3. What have others done? 4. What is
still missing? 5. What is your solution? 6. Does it work?

================================================================== PART
2 – BEFORE WRITING
==================================================================

Collect: - IEEE template - Research papers - Notes from each paper -
Figures - Screenshots - Architecture diagrams - References

Create folders: 01_Papers 02_Images 03_Diagrams 04_References 05_Drafts
06_Final

================================================================== PART
3 – TITLE RULES
==================================================================

Good titles are: - Specific - Technical - Concise - Searchable

Good example: Asset Tokenization Platform for Fractional Ownership of
Real-World Assets Using Ethereum Smart Contracts

Avoid: - A Study on… - Project Report - Mini Project - Final Year
Project

================================================================== PART
4 – ABSTRACT CHECKLIST
==================================================================

Order: 1. Background 2. Problem 3. Proposed system 4. Technologies 5.
Results 6. Conclusion

Never include: - Citations - Equations - Bullet points - Tables -
Figures

================================================================== PART
5 – INTRODUCTION
==================================================================

Suggested flow: Paragraph 1: Importance of blockchain and digital
assets.

Paragraph 2: Problems with traditional asset ownership.

Paragraph 3: Need for tokenization.

Paragraph 4: Project objectives.

Paragraph 5: Paper contributions.

================================================================== PART
6 – LITERATURE SURVEY
==================================================================

For every paper: - Citation - Objective - Method - Findings -
Limitations - Relevance

Never criticize without evidence.

End with: Research Gap: Explain exactly what existing work lacks and how
your work addresses it.

================================================================== PART
7 – METHODOLOGY
==================================================================

Explain: Input → Validation → Asset Registration → Smart Contract
Deployment → Token Generation → Purchase Flow → Ownership Update →
Portfolio

Do not mix implementation details with methodology.

================================================================== PART
8 – IMPLEMENTATION
==================================================================

Describe: Frontend Backend Database Blockchain Wallet Integration
Testing

================================================================== PART
9 – RESULTS
==================================================================

Present only verified results.

Include: - Screenshots - Performance observations - Comparison tables -
Limitations

Never fabricate benchmarks.

================================================================== PART
10 – FIGURES
==================================================================

Rules: - Number sequentially. - Use clear captions. - Mention every
figure in text. - Maintain consistent style.

================================================================== PART
11 – TABLES
==================================================================

Rules: - Caption above table. - Units where applicable. - Consistent
formatting.

================================================================== PART
12 – REFERENCES
==================================================================

Use IEEE numbering.

Reference every non-original idea.

================================================================== PART
13 – WRITING STYLE
==================================================================

Use: “The proposed system…” “This study…” “The platform…”

Avoid: “I” “We” “You”

Keep paragraphs between 80 and 150 words where practical.

================================================================== PART
14 – COMMON REASONS IEEE PAPERS ARE REJECTED
==================================================================

-   No novelty.
-   Weak literature review.
-   Poor English.
-   Missing citations.
-   Unsupported claims.
-   Incomplete evaluation.
-   Formatting errors.
-   Poor figures.
-   Plagiarism.
-   Inconsistent terminology.
