🔥 CORE CONCEPT QUESTIONS
1. What is Asset Tokenization?

Answer:
Asset tokenization is the process of converting ownership of a real-world asset into digital tokens on a blockchain. Each token represents a fraction of the asset, allowing multiple users to own a part of it.

2. Why is asset tokenization needed?

Answer:
Traditional assets like real estate are illiquid and require large capital. Tokenization enables fractional ownership, improves liquidity, reduces entry barriers, and increases accessibility for small investors.

3. What types of assets can be tokenized?

Answer:

Real estate
Gold and commodities
Art and collectibles
Stocks and bonds
Intellectual property
4. What problem are you solving?

Answer:
The project solves the problem of limited access and low liquidity in high-value assets by enabling fractional ownership through blockchain technology.

6. What is the role of blockchain in your project?

Answer:
Blockchain ensures secure, transparent, and immutable storage of ownership and transaction data. It removes the need for intermediaries and prevents data tampering.

7. Why did you use Ethereum?

Answer:
Ethereum supports smart contracts, has strong developer support, and is widely used for decentralized applications, making it suitable for implementing tokenization.

8. What are smart contracts?

Answer:
Smart contracts are self-executing programs stored on the blockchain that automatically perform actions like token transfer when predefined conditions are met.

🧠 ARCHITECTURE QUESTIONS
10. Explain your system architecture

Answer:
The system consists of four layers:

Frontend (React) for user interaction
Backend (Node.js) for API handling
Database (MongoDB) for off-chain data
Blockchain (Ethereum) for token transactions and ownership
11. Why do you need a backend if you have blockchain?

Answer:
Blockchain stores only essential data like ownership and transactions. The backend handles user authentication, asset details, and fast data retrieval, improving performance and usability.

12. What is stored on-chain vs off-chain?

Answer:

On-chain: ownership, token transactions
Off-chain: user data, asset details, images, transaction history
13. What is the role of MetaMask?

Answer:
MetaMask acts as a wallet that allows users to connect to the blockchain, sign transactions, and interact with smart contracts securely.
